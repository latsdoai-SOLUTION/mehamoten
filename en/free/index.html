What worked best

The gym and the spaceship. Places everyone knows, with one small, clear problem. People started laughing without much explanation.

What we changed afterwards

Fewer instructions on the screen, a first line for each person, and the game decides who is who. That way nobody gets stuck at the start.

What stayed exactly the same

No scores and no losers in the default mode. You don't need to be funny. The person who listens to their partner does well.
