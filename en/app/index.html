Welcome to Mehamoten

An improv game for a group. You don't need to be funny and you don't need perfect English. Just react. The digital host guides you every step.

🃏

Get a scene

Each round the host gives two players a place, who is who, and a first line each. Say your line, then just talk like you are really there.

🔔

"Swap!"

In the middle of a scene the host may call "Swap!". You switch characters and keep going from the same point. Or one player gets a small task on the screen.

💡

Stuck? There's help

A hint button, and a Stuck? button that gives you a line to say. Nobody is left alone on stage.

🏢

For teams: Team mode

Your company name on the screen, cards for your industry, a guided closing talk at the end, and a session summary you can send to the manager.

📖 Facilitator guide

No improv experience needed. The app tells you what to do. You just read out loud and press buttons.

Who is the facilitator?

The facilitator holds the phone or stands by the screen and presses the buttons. They don't have to play. Each round two players go up to the "stage" (any free space in the room), and everyone else is the audience.

Mixed groups and English level

This version is made for teams with different levels of English. The scenes are everyday situations, the instructions are short, and many tasks use the body and the face more than words. Simple English is perfect. Gestures, sounds and made-up words are all welcome. Say this out loud at the start: "You don't need perfect English. The goal is to have fun together."

Lite or Full?

There are two styles (in game setup, "Round style"). 🟢 Lite is the default, and the best choice for a first game. Each round has one everyday stage, the screen shows each player's role and gives a first line to each player. The first round has no interruptions. From the second round there is one thing in the middle: "Swap!" (switch characters and continue) or a small task for one of the two. Every fourth round is something different that breaks the pattern. 🔵 Full is the advanced version, with twists, extra events and lots of swaps.

🔥 Warm-up

Before the first round the whole room does 3 short tasks at the same time: one without words, one in pairs with the person next to you, and one for the whole group out loud. Nobody stands alone in front of an audience, and everything works sitting down. Read the screen and press "Start". A task doesn't fit the room? "Different task". You can always skip. During the game, on the screen between rounds, there is a 🔥 Warm-up button with more tasks, for example when the energy drops.

One round, step by step

  1. 🎤 Who goes up. The screen shows two names. They go to the stage.
  2. 🃏 The scene. The screen shows the place, the small problem, who is who, and the first line for each player. Read it out loud. Don't like it? "Draw again".
  3. ⏱️ The scene runs. Countdown: 3, 2, 1, Action! The players improvise while the timer runs. If you hear "Swap!", they switch characters and continue. If a task appears, the named player does it.
  4. 💡 Help. Use Hint or Stuck? when players need help getting started.
  5. 👏 End of scene. Big applause! Then "Next round".
  6. 🏁 End of the game. In Team mode there is also a 10-minute closing talk, step by step: a moment from the game, whether it connects to work, and what we take with us. The app tells you what to say at each step. At the end there is a short summary for the manager, with no scores and no names.

Tips for the facilitator

  • There are no mistakes in improv. Everything said on stage becomes "true", and you continue from it.
  • The key rule: "Yes, and..." Accept what your partner said, and add to it. Don't say "no".
  • Mix the pairs. Put together colleagues who don't usually work together. That's where the best moments happen.
  • The first round is the scariest. It helps if the facilitator and someone brave go first.
  • Applause after every scene, even if it didn't work. That gives confidence to the next players.
