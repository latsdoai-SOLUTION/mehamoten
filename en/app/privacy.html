Privacy policy: Mehamoten

Last updated: November 2026

"Mehamoten" (the "app") is a digital party game. We take your privacy seriously. This page explains what data the app collects. The short answer: almost nothing.

In short: the app does not collect, send or share personal information. Everything you type (player names, settings, scores) is saved only on your device, and is never sent to a server.

1. Data we collect

The app does not collect personally identifying information. No sign-up, no account, no tracking.

The following data is saved only locally on the device (in the browser's local storage) and never leaves the device:

  • The player names you type.
  • Preferences and settings (game mode, host personality, voice, sound, hints, accessibility).
  • Scores and progress of the current game, the round log for the session summary, cards you added yourself, and a simple stats counter (how many games, rounds and swaps were played on the device).
  • If entered: company name, company logo and team license code.

When several groups play on the same device, the "New group" button deletes the names, scores and company name of the previous group, so the next group starts with a clean screen. If the device was not used for more than six hours, the app offers to start a new group, and opening a link for a different company starts with a clean screen.

You can delete all data at any time via ⚙️ Settings → "Reset data", by clearing the site data in your browser, or by uninstalling the app.

2. Permissions

The app may use the device's speaker / audio output to play the host's voice and sound effects. It does not ask for access to the camera, microphone, contacts, location or files.

3. Voice (text to speech)

In the English version, the host's voice is produced by your browser's or operating system's built-in speech engine. The spoken text is fixed game text and the player names you typed. It does not include other personal information.

4. Fonts and network

The app may load fonts from Google Fonts on first launch (after that it works fully offline). Other than that, the app does not talk to external servers.

5. Children

The app is family friendly. Since we collect no personal information at all, we collect no information from children.

6. Ads and analytics

The app has no ads, uses no third party analytics, and has no tracking components. (The marketing website mehamoten.com may use Google Analytics for visit statistics. The app does not.)

7. Changes

If we update this policy, we will update the date at the top of this page.
